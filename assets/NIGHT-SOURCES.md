## Changes

Both delivery JPEGs were resized to 3000 pixels wide with bicubic interpolation and re-encoded at JPEG quality 91. Runtime presentation applies viewport cropping, perspective camera movement, subtle foreground UV parallax, exposure/tone mapping and bloom. No buildings or image detail were generated. The photos are historical imagery, not live views; their animation is photo-based 2.5D, not reconstructed city geometry.

변경 사항: 가로 3000픽셀 축소 및 JPEG 재압축, 화면 비율에 따른 크롭, 원근 카메라 이동, 전경 시차 효과, 밝기·톤 매핑 및 빛 번짐 적용.
